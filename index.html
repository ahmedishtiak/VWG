The engine

A small, single-turbo, direct-injection petrol engine. Cast-iron block, aluminium head, two camshafts driven by a chain, variable timing on the intake cam. The intercooler sits inside the intake manifold and is cooled by water, not air.

Air filter→Turbo→Throttle→Water-cooled intercooler→Cylinders→Turbine→Catalyst

The gearbox

The DQ200 is two manual gearboxes in one case, each with its own dry clutch. One handles odd gears, the other even gears and reverse. A computer with its own hydraulics (the mechatronic unit) shifts them. The next gear is already selected before you need it, so shifts are quick.

Your car's mechatronic unit has already been replaced, which removes the biggest DQ200 risk. Clutch wear is still something to watch.

Watch first on this car

  • Timing chain rattle for 2–5 seconds on a cold start
  • Coolant level dropping with no visible leak (intercooler or water pump)
  • Loss of power / P0299: turbo wastegate
  • Shudder pulling away in 1st or reverse: DSG clutch
  • Oil consumption: check the dipstick every few fill-ups

Oil: fixed or LongLife?

With VW 504 00 oil the car can run the flexible LongLife interval, up to 30 000 km or 2 years. With VW 502 00 oil it's fixed at 15 000 km or 1 year. On a timing-chain TSI with short winter trips, change it every 10 000–15 000 km or once a year. Clean oil is the cheapest protection the chain tensioner can get.

Useful to know

  • Oil filter: spin-on canister at the front of the engine
  • Check oil with the engine warm, on level ground, about 5 minutes after switching off
  • Don't overfill. Stay between the MIN and MAX marks
  • Wheel bolts: 120 Nm. Re-torque after 50–100 km
  • Fuel: 95 E10 is fine. 98 E5 can reduce knock and carbon on a hard-worked car
